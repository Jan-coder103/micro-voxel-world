# Phase 10 — Reusable weapons and explosions

Add a dedicated Weapons lab sharing the flight scene/controller; preserve Flight lab's fixture and resource baseline. Prototype static, resettable range targets rather than Phase 11 zombies. Airborne targets let level forward fire demonstrate damage; a ground cluster supports dive attacks. Targets do not collide with aircraft and do not persist.

Data definitions provide minigun (20 Hz), autocannon (5 Hz) and explosive shell (1 Hz), each with range/damage/effect/audio identity. All use authoritative instantaneous swept rays, not simulated ballistic projectiles. Tracers are cosmetic. Unlimited ammunition and zero spread make cadence and aiming inspectable. Per-weapon tick cooldowns prevent selection bypass. Weapons advance only on successful 60 Hz flight ticks, so pause/crash and dropped catchup cannot keep firing.

A static 32 m spatial hash indexes target spheres. Ray broadphase queries only cells in the segment envelope, then finds the nearest exact ray/sphere hit. Terrain uses seeded height samples at <=0.25 m with a conservative 0.15 m clearance; it blocks direct fire and blast visibility even when render chunks are unavailable. No buildings exist in this range. Target hulls block direct fire; dead targets disappear. Blasts do not use target hulls as shields. Blast falloff is linear by target-centre distance (full damage at centre, zero at radius); each live target receives damage at most once per explosion, and terrain blocks centre visibility. No direct damage is added to explosive damage. No attacks against offscreen population descriptors exist yet.

A fixed 128-slot visual pool holds tracers, muzzle flashes, impacts and expanding blasts; full pools drop only cosmetics. Shared target/effect geometry and materials have explicit ownership. No persistent debris, per-shot geometry/light allocation, terrain mutation or enemies. A Web Audio oscillator/gain envelope gives distinct shot/explosion sounds with at most eight voices, master mute, user-gesture activation and visible unavailable/blocked status. Presentation may coalesce shot sounds per frame without changing damage; pause/reset/teardown stop voices.

Controls: Space fires; 1/2/3 or selector chooses weapon; Recover / Reset restores aircraft, targets, counters and effects. Viewport focus scopes firing and selection; switching page controls pauses flight. Audio defaults muted. No mouse aim, recoil/spread simulation, ammo/heat, projectile gravity, rockets/bombs or final audio assets in this phase.
