# Phase 12 — Clearpass combat prototype and measurement gate

Combine the existing flight, weapon, horde, streaming, persistence and audio systems at /play/, while retaining all six earlier regression entry points. Use the original authored attack-aircraft mesh at source scale, and existing instanced zombies. No new assets/dependencies or later world-generation/mission-progression systems.

One sortie: clear the three encounters (-1,0), (0,0), (1,0), visit within 60 m of the sector at <=70 m AGL, then physically return within 20 m of the base at (16,192). Default seed 12012 is separate from lab seed 103. Spawn at 40 m/s, 32 m AGL, heading north. Shells are default; minigun/autocannon remain selectable. Default aim assist aims at a living objective member within 70 degrees of the nose and 240 m. Manual mode fires along the nose. Lock/waypoint markers and progress/flight HUD explain target acquisition and navigation.

Return assist is an explicit optional bank/pitch/throttle controller after clearance; it flies the same fixed-step model, cannot teleport or complete without entering the base zone, and manual input cancels it. Crashes fail the current attempt. Recover restores the approach/ready state and retains persisted wounds/deaths, but resets the visit requirement so it cannot teleport to victory. Reload restores population, not aircraft/attempt state. Already cleared sectors still require a reconnaissance flyover and return. No rewards/unlocks/save migration; those are later work.

Flight gains an optional swept static-obstacle query. The prototype expands horde wall AABBs by 9 m for the authored aircraft envelope, with 9 m terrain clearance for the authored aircraft at every attitude. The occupied source cells plus voxel corners are tested to fit that envelope. Earlier primitive-flight fixtures retain their original 3 m ground clearance. Logical collision is independent of workers. A reusable 1,024 m-wide coarse terrain underlay (32 m samples, 2.5 m below the field) covers detail-loading gaps and the horizon without replacing authoritative terrain. It moves in 64 m increments and reuses GPU buffers. A two-chunk forward look-ahead biases the bounded streamed patch and horde residency toward the attack run, while retaining the aircraft in the patch. Streamed terrain remains bounded to 49 chunks/two jobs-results/two integrations. The underlay is visual fallback, not collision or advanced generation.

Reproducible isolated benchmark routes at /play/?benchmark=crowd and ?benchmark=fire use fresh in-memory population without writing player records. They fly a deterministic orbit; fire mode continuously fires the minigun into available crowds. Warmup frames are excluded; bounded sample histories record real rAF intervals, CPU simulation/presentation/submission time, dropped simulation time, populations and resource/work caps. Reports can be downloaded as JSON. A 75% render-scale preset and ?scale=0.75 benchmark variant compare the software-renderer fill bottleneck against 100%; populations/collision stay unchanged. CPU submission is not GPU frame time. A Node fixed-tick benchmark supplies repeatable CPU/load invariants independently of browser rendering. Declared device/browser/renderer and resolution accompany all measurements; no inferred hardware FPS guarantee.

Automated completion, collision/recovery/save/resource tests and measured scenarios establish implementation evidence. Player enjoyment, approach/aiming/hit-feedback/recovery feedback and a physical GPU reference remain explicit verification gates. Do not start Phase 13 merely because automated tests pass.
